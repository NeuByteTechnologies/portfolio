About Me

I’m Gordon Neuls — a Systems Analyst with a strong technical foundation in SQL, data modeling, and enterprise integration. My work centers on understanding how systems behave, documenting workflows, and guiding modernization efforts that make platforms more reliable, maintainable, and scalable. I focus on clarity, structure, and long‑term sustainability in every system I touch, supported by strong visual grammar and consistent documentation practices.

Engineering Philosophy

My approach to engineering is shaped by a few core principles:

  • Documentation is a first-class artifact. A system isn’t complete until it’s understood.
  • Consistency beats cleverness. Clean, predictable patterns scale better than shortcuts.
  • Visual grammar matters. Diagrams, UI flows, and architecture visuals are part of the product.
  • Systems should feel intentional. Every component should have a purpose and a place.
